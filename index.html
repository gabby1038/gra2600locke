Welcome to the World of Sonny Angels

Sonny Angels come in many different styles and themes. Each collection has its own unique designs, making it fun for collectors to discover new series and find ones they love.

A Little More About the Collection

Collecting Sonny Angels is a fun hobby that allows you to express your creativity and love for these adorable little angels. It's very rewarding to discover the secret figures and Robby's hidden within different series.

Each collector will usually have a main series that they focus on collecting. Or they might enjoy collecting across multiple series, depending on their personal preferences. Me personally I have many from one series but won't complete the set since I don't care for the rest of them. This is why some collectors have the entire series and some pick only the ones they want like me! This is totally okay! Please don't feel pressured to complete every series if it doesn't bring you joy. Collecting should be fun and personal, not stressful.
