About Sonny Angels

Learn more about these adorable collectible figures!

What Are Sonny Angels?

Sonny Angels are small collectible figures known for their cute designs and different themed costumes. They come in many different styles, making each series unique and fun to collect.

What Makes Them Special?

One of the fun things about Sonny Angels is that you never know exactly which figure you might get when opening a blind box. Collectors enjoy discovering new designs and finding their favorite characters.
